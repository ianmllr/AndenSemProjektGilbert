Frequently Asked Questions

What is our purpose?

Our purpose is to provide a platform for people to easily and conveniently buy and sell used fashion items of high quality.


How long does shipping take?

That depends on the seller. Once a seller has received the payment from the buyer, they will send the item to the buyer.


How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs depend on the location of the buyer and the seller. We will contact the buyer to confirm the shipping cost.


How much does it cost to list an item?

Listing an item on our platform is free. Once it is sold, we will take 8% of the sale price.


What is the return policy?

If an item is returned, we will refund the buyer the full amount of the sale price.


How do I know if an item is authentic?

For a fee, you can have the item sent to us for authentication. If it verified to be authentic, we will send it to you. If it is found to be fake, you will be refunded the whole price.
